The upstream Jest community is working hard to make the testing experience even greater. These community resources are part of the general Jest ecosystem and are not operated by the independently maintained `@pkg-nec/jest` fork.

[jest-community](https://github.com/jest-community) is an upstream GitHub organization for high quality Jest additions curated by upstream Jest maintainers and collaborators. It already features some of our favorite projects, to name a few:

- [vscode-jest](https://github.com/jest-community/vscode-jest)
- [jest-extended](https://github.com/jest-community/jest-extended)
- [eslint-plugin-jest](https://github.com/jest-community/eslint-plugin-jest)
- [awesome-jest](https://github.com/jest-community/awesome-jest)

Community projects under one organization are a great way for the upstream Jest ecosystem to experiment with new ideas, techniques, and approaches. Encourage contributions from the community and publish contributions independently at a faster pace.

## Awesome Jest

The upstream jest-community org maintains an [awesome-jest](https://github.com/jest-community/awesome-jest) list of great projects and resources related to Jest.

If you have something awesome to share, feel free to contribute to the upstream awesome-jest list ([send a PR here](https://github.com/jest-community/awesome-jest/pulls)). If you would like to transfer your project to the upstream jest-community org, reach out to one of its owners.
